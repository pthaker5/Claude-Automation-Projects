# Module 00 — Math & Probability Foundations

**Status:** LIVE — deep material in [modules/module-00-math-probability/](../modules/module-00-math-probability/)
**Weight (QR):** ★★★ — this is the single most-screened area in quant interviews.

## Learning objectives

By the end you can:
- Solve interview-grade probability brainteasers *out loud*, under time pressure, explaining
  your reasoning (combinatorics, conditioning, symmetry, indicators, linearity of expectation).
- Work fluently with random variables: expectation, variance, covariance/correlation, moment
  generating intuition, common distributions and where each shows up in markets.
- State and *use* the Law of Large Numbers and the Central Limit Theorem — and articulate
  precisely why daily returns violate CLT-based intuition (fat tails, dependence, vol clustering).
- Do the linear algebra that quant work actually uses: matrix multiplication as composition,
  eigendecomposition, positive semi-definiteness, covariance matrices, a first look at PCA.

## Topics

1. Probability spaces, counting, independence
2. Conditional probability, Bayes' rule, law of total probability
3. Random variables, expectation, variance; linearity and the indicator trick
4. Common distributions (Bernoulli/Binomial, Geometric, Poisson, Uniform, Exponential, Normal,
   Lognormal, Student-t) with market interpretations
5. Joint distributions, covariance, correlation and its traps
6. LLN, CLT, and empirical reality of returns (fat tails, skew, kurtosis)
7. Linear algebra refresher: vectors, matrices, rank, eigen-things, PSD, covariance matrices
8. Brainteaser technique toolbox — running thread through the whole module
